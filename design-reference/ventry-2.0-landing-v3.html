Questions people ask

When do organisers get paid?

Each working day's sales are settled the next working day, net of the 3% platform fee. No waiting until after the event.

What does Verified mean?

The organiser's identity has been checked, and venue documentation was reviewed before the event went live. A photo of a venue is not accepted as proof of a booking.

I lost my ticket. What now?

Recover it yourself at ventrybooking.com/retrieve using the details you bought with, or ask the support chat and it will be sent back to your purchase email.

Who handles refunds?

The organiser sets and pays refunds under the policy published on their event page. Ventry collects and passes on claims, and refunds anything it still holds when an event is cancelled.

What does it cost to list?

Nothing upfront. No listing fee, no subscription. Ventry takes 3% at settlement, so if nothing sells, nothing is owed.
