Core principle, non-negotiable: organisers never see buyer email addresses. Anywhere. On their dashboard, in exports, in the audience list — names only. The email relationship belongs to Ventry. This is a product decision, not an oversight.

Goal 1 — Checkout consent.
Two optional, unticked checkboxes at checkout, exact copy:

Box 1 (organiser): "I agree to receive updates and offers from the event organiser, sent through Ventry. You can unsubscribe at any time."
Box 2 (Ventry): "I agree to receive emails from Ventry about upcoming events, new features, and platform updates. You can unsubscribe at any time."
Consents stored per buyer with timestamps. Organiser consent is scoped to that organiser.

Goal 2 — Audience tab.
Problem: organisers have no view of who has opted into hearing from them.
Outcome: a new "Audience" section on the organiser dashboard (people-style icon) listing everyone who ticked Box 1 at checkout plus everyone who tapped Notify Me on their organiser page. Show the name the person used and where they came from (ticket purchase vs Notify Me), and a total count. No email addresses, no export containing emails.

Goal 3 — Contact Audience composer.
Problem: organisers need to reach that audience but only through Ventry.
Outcome: a "Contact Audience" area where the organiser composes an email — subject, body, images attached the same way flyers attach to ticket emails. Submitting does NOT send; it enters a review queue. Organiser sees the status of each mail: pending review, approved/sent, rejected (with reason).

Goal 4 — Admin review queue.
Problem: one bad mail from one organiser damages the sending domain for everyone.
Outcome: in admin, a queue of submitted newsletters with full preview. Admin approves (triggers send) or rejects with a reason sent back to the organiser. Nothing sends without approval.

Goal 5 — Sending.
On approval, the mail goes via Resend to the organiser's full audience (consented buyers + Notify Me subscribers), from the organiser's display name, reply-to support@ventrybooking.com. Every mail carries a working unsubscribe link that removes that person from that organiser's audience (Box 1/Notify Me) without touching their Ventry-level consent, and vice versa for Ventry mails. After the send completes, the organiser receives a confirmation email from Ventry that their mail reached their audience.

Goal 6 — Billing-ready architecture.
Free now, but later this becomes a yearly subscription or pay-per-mail. Structure it so metering/gating is a policy check, not a rewrite: an entitlement or send-credit check at the submission point that currently always passes. Record sends per organiser so usage-based pricing has data from day one.

Done when: a test buyer ticks Box 1 → appears in the organiser's Audience by name only → organiser composes a mail with an image → it lands in the admin queue → admin approves → mail arrives from the organiser's display name with reply-to support@ and a working unsubscribe → organiser receives the confirmation email → the send is logged against the organiser. And a rejected mail shows the rejection reason on the organiser dashboard.
